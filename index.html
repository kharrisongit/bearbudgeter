How to use this

BearBudgeter plans debt payments and savings from the cash you have available. Avalanche targets the highest APR; Snowball targets the smallest balance; Squasher halves the three largest remaining balances on a bonus check and pays minimums on the rest. On checks without a bonus, it halves the smaller balances and pays minimums on the three largest. Accounts are ranked again each paycheck, including Affirm and loans. Tap Squash on the paycheck you want to use to finish the remaining debt. It targets full payoff using available money after your budget and savings, with or without a bonus. Tap Undo Squash to restore the usual payments. Squash never turns on automatically.

Minimum payments come first, savings contributions second, then extra debt payments. Shortfalls are shown explicitly. Squasher leaves money unallocated once its targets are met.

Getting started

1. Open your setup. Use Income and Budget on Overview, then open the Accounts tab to add your balances.

2. Set your income. Open "💰 Income" and fill in your hourly rate, hours per paycheck, and next check date. Tap "Use This Estimate" to set your default take-home pay. You can still adjust any individual check later.

3. Enter your accounts. Open "💳 Accounts" and tap "+ Add Account" for each card or loan — balance, APR, and minimum payment. Tap an account afterward to edit those details or see its payoff progress. It automatically figures out which account to attack first, no need to sort them yourself.

4. Enter your budget. Open "🧾 Budget" and fill in your regular expenses. Whatever's left over is what goes toward your debt.

5. Hit Recalculate. Your Paychecks tab updates instantly, showing exactly what to pay on each account, every paycheck, until you're debt-free.

6. Check off paychecks as you go. Once you've made a round of payments, tap the checkbox on that paycheck's card. It'll gray out and sink to the bottom of the full schedule so you always know where you left off. Accounts that are already paid off drop out of future paycheck cards automatically. Tap the ↺ button above your paycheck plan to uncheck everything at once if you need a clean slate.

7. Adjust anything on a specific check. The paycheck amount, every budget line item, and savings contributions are all editable under "Paycheck & budget" on that paycheck's card — tap the number and type in what actually happened, and it updates that check and future projections. Completed checks stay locked.

Features

Avalanche vs. Snowball. Avalanche targets your highest-interest debt first, saving the most money overall. Snowball targets your smallest balance first, so you clear whole accounts faster. Switch anytime in Your payoff playbook on Overview. Only future payments change.

Debt breakdown pie. Tap the small pie chart on your Debt Tracker card to drop down a bigger one showing exactly how your remaining balance splits across each account.

Savings goals. Tap "🐷 Start Tracking Savings" where your Debt Tracker card is to add your first goal. Each goal card has ➕ (add another), ⚙️ (edit), and 🗑️ (delete, if you have more than one) in its top-right corner — tap "Save" at the bottom of the edit panel when you're done to close it back up. Delete your only goal and you're back to that same start button.

Editable budget line items. Any regular budget expense can be adjusted for just one paycheck — tap its amount right on that paycheck's card if it came in higher or lower than usual that time.

One-time expenses. Something extra come up? Open "Paycheck & budget" and tap "🧾 Add One-Time Expense" on a paycheck card to add a one-off cost that only affects that single check, without changing your regular recurring budget.

Bonus calculator. Tap "🎁 Add Bonus" on any paycheck and enter the gross amount. Estimated take-home and Squasher payments update automatically for that check only. Bonuses never recur.

Accounts page. Shows how much has actually gone toward each debt account so far, based only on the paychecks you've checked off — separate from the projected future schedule. Tap an account (or its pie slice) to see a full breakdown or edit its balance, APR, and minimum payment. Its pie chart and stats track the current payoff cycle only; tap "📜 History" for three separate sections — lifetime interest paid across all cycles, every payoff date, and the payment ledger (shows the last 2 payments, with a button to see the rest).

Account paid off milestone. The paycheck card where an individual account hits zero gets its own celebration banner and highlighted border, just like the debt-free and savings-goal-reached cards do. Checking off that paycheck — or the one where a savings goal hits its target — also takes over the screen with a confetti celebration — tap anywhere or wait a few seconds for it to clear.

Bear Run. A little Chrome-Dino-style game on its own card — tap it (or press Space/Up) to jump the bear over whatever's coming, tap again to start a new run after a miss. Your best score is saved on this device. The obstacles and bear's outfit switch with your holiday theme below.

Bear Crush. A small match-3 game right below Bear Run — tap a treat, then tap an adjacent one to swap them; matching 3 or more in a row clears them and scores points, with chain reactions worth extra. The ↺ button starts a fresh board any time, and your best score is saved on this device. The treats switch with your holiday theme too.

Bear Quest. A small top-down adventure below Bear Crush — move the bear with the on-screen pad (or arrow keys/WASD) and tap ⚔️ (or Space/X) to swing at critters. Clear every enemy in a room to advance to the next wave, which adds one more enemy each time. You've got 3 hearts — getting touched costs one, with a brief flash of invincibility right after — and an occasional ❤️ pickup can heal you back up. The ↺ button starts a fresh run, and your best wave reached is saved on this device. The critters switch with your holiday theme too.

Bear Catch. A small explore-and-battle adventure below Bear Quest. You start in Bear Hollow, home to two buildings: the Lab (🔬), where Bear Professor Hazel lets you pick one of 3 starter bears to partner with — it's added straight to your Dex — and home (🏠), where you can say goodbye to Mom before heading out (she'll point you at the Lab first if you haven't picked a bear yet). The Professor explains the whole quest up front: catch all 18 bear variants, beat every Gym Leader and the Elite Four, then dethrone the Bear King. Only after picking a starter can you leave town. From there it's 32 sections: 9 zones, each made of two consecutive Wild Areas (a shallower approach trail, then a deeper stretch right before town — each with its own distinct layout, a wandering trainer, and a couple of NPCs to chat with along the way) leading into a City, then 4 separate Elite Four chambers walked through one at a time, then the Bear King's Chamber. Move with the on-screen pad (played as a human Cub Catcher, not a bear); step into grass for a chance at a wild encounter — 18 catchable variants total, two native to each zone, each with a level that climbs the further you travel. Every caught bear can lead — tap 🔄 any time from the overworld to pick which one battles for you (each gives the shared Cub level a different stat bonus, and the panel shows the Cub's current HP), or tap 🔄 Switch mid-battle to swap in a fresh one (it costs your turn, so the enemy gets a free hit, same as a real swap) — the same panel also lets you drink a Healing Potion or Full Restore. Every City is a Plaza with more to see than just the Battle Hall: chat with the Elder and a couple of townsfolk (each with a bear companion of their own, though the bears don't talk) for local color — and they'll actually react once you've beaten their Gym Leader, or once you've become Bear King — then step through actual doors — same as the Battle Hall's — into the Shopkeeper's General Store (🏪, now with its own shelves and counter) to buy Honey Traps and healing items, the Historian's Archive (🧑‍🏫, with bookshelves and a reading table) for a deeper bit of local trivia, and Nurse Willow's Healing Center (🏥, with real beds) to heal your Cub for free — she'll always ask first before healing. Each of those three buildings has a second NPC of its own to chat with, not just its specialist. Every trainer and Gym Leader (and every wild/trainer bear's level) is shown before or during the fight, and beating any of them earns coins. Battles are turn-based: ⚔️ Attack, 🍯 Catch a wild bear (costs one Honey Trap — better odds the lower its HP, buy more at any Store when you run low), or 🏃 Run — you can't catch or run from a trainer battle, though. Healing is no longer automatic: beating a trainer still levels your Cub up and pays out coins, but it won't top off your HP, and fainting limps you back with just 1 HP — you'll need Nurse Willow or a healing item to recover. Healing Potions fully heal until your 3rd Gym Badge; after that they only restore half, and a pricier Full Restore item (sold once you've earned that badge) takes over as the on-the-go full heal. Beating a City's Gym Leader unlocks the path (▶, or 🔒 if still locked) onward. Clear all 9 Gym Leaders to reach the Elite Four, beat all four of them to reach the Bear King — beating him makes you the new Bear King, but he only hands over the ultra-rare 19th bear, the Celestial Bear, if you've also caught all 18 other variants (catch the last one later and it's still yours the moment you complete the set). Once you actually own the Celestial Bear, Professor Hazel herself becomes available back in Bear Hollow — the true final battle, and the toughest in the game. Tap 📖 any time to see the full Bear Dex — how many variants exist and which you've caught. Your collection, Cub's level and HP, coins, Honey Traps, healing items, badges, and current section are all saved on this device — the ↺ button fully resets your Bear Catch progress (with an in-app confirmation first), sending you back to square one in Bear Hollow.

Pro tips

• Savings contributions come out of what's left after your budget and funded minimum payments, before extra debt payments — raising a contribution slows your payoff timeline a bit.

• Check off paychecks promptly. Several things (remaining totals, savings progress, the accounts page) are based on the most recently checked paycheck, not the calendar date.

• Delete a savings goal any time with its 🗑️ icon; "Reset Everything" in Personal setup wipes debt, budget, income, and savings alike — use it carefully.

• Choose whether each minimum is monthly or per paycheck. Payments are limited to available cash; a shortage is displayed. Interest can still grow a balance if the payment is too small.

A little progress goes a long way. 🐾
